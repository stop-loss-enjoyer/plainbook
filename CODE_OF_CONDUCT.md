# Code of conduct

Plainbook is a small project with one maintainer. The rules for talking here
are short.

- Be civil. Disagree with the idea, never with the person.
- Stay on the subject of the issue or the pull request.
- No harassment, insults, sexual content or personal attacks, in any form and
  towards anyone.
- Do not post anyone's trades, balances or personal details, your own
  included. A synthetic example shows a problem just as well.
- No selling: signals, courses, brokers, prop firms and referral links are
  removed without discussion.

Comments that break these rules are edited or deleted, and repeated or serious
breaches end in a block from the repository.

To report something you would rather not raise in public, use **Report a
vulnerability** on the Security tab: it reaches the maintainer privately, and
it is fine to use it for conduct as well as for security.
